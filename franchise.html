Questions

Quick answers

What is the FOCO model?

FOCO means Franchise-Owned, Company-Operated. You hold the territory rights while RoadMate runs technology, operations, marketing, staffing and training. You do not need a shop or any stock.

Do I need a physical outlet?

No. The partnership is fully remote and you do not run a store.

What does RoadMate do?

RoadMate helps retailers, distributors and manufacturers add quick commerce to their traditional business, connecting them with customers on one platform.

Where do the numbers come from?

The 12,000+ shops, 100+ manufacturers and 5 lakh+ products delivered come from RoadMate’s live automobile network. Quick commerce across grocery, restaurant, sports and textiles is launching soon.
