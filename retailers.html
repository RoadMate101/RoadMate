Keep your shop. Add quick commerce.

Benefits

A big supporting hand for every shop owner.

You built the trust, the location and the customer relationships. RoadMate adds the technology, the customers and the supply support.

Orders on your phone

New customer orders arrive in the Shop app with a clear accept or decline, so you stay in control of every order.

0% commission on sales

Your margins stay with you. RoadMate does not take a commission on shop sales.

New customers nearby

Customers around you find and order from your shop, in addition to your walk-in footfall.

Easy restocking

Order stock from your distributor or manufacturer inside the app and see when the delivery will arrive.

Brand deals and offers

Get offers directly from manufacturers, plus cross-brand collaborations to promote.

Local support

Your regional partner verifies your shop and helps you get set up and started.
