The Company

From Kerala's largest auto-services platform to a multi-industry quick-commerce brand for India and the world.

RoadMate is built by Nextwave Access Private Limited — here's the story, the team, and the track record behind it.

RoadMate is built by Nextwave Access Private Limited, a Kochi-based technology company incorporated in April 2021 and recognised by DPIIT and the Kerala Startup Mission. Nextwave's first product — RoadMate for automobiles — grew into Kerala's largest multi-brand auto-services and B2B marketplace, onboarding 12,000+ shops and 100+ manufacturers onto a single manufacturer-distributor-retailer-customer platform.

RoadMate is now extending that exact playbook beyond automobiles — into groceries, restaurants, sports and textiles — with quick-commerce operations launching soon. As quick commerce raises customer expectations everywhere, RoadMate gives local retailers, distributors and manufacturers across India a genuine route to add fast, convenient service to their traditional business, and to grow with the market.

The opportunity is offered to the market through a structured, franchise-style partnership network — a defined role, an exclusive territory and a clear, scalable route to regional and pan-India expansion, while RoadMate centrally manages technology, operations and brand. RoadMate is being built as a global product, connecting markets across India and the world on one single platform.
