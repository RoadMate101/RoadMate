For Distributors

Every shop you supply, on one platform.

As retailers move to digital ordering, distributors need to move with them. RoadMate gives you order-taking, credit and dues tracking, dispatch and driver tools in one place, so you stay at the centre of the supply chain.

Start Onboarding See the App
Benefits

Stay at the centre of the supply chain.

RoadMate gives distributors the tools to serve retailers faster, collect on time and run deliveries without paperwork.

All your shops in one place

See every shop you supply with its credit terms, dues and recent orders.

Digital order-taking

Your field team books orders in the app. Retailers can also order directly.

Dues and collections

See who owes what and when, record payments by cash, bank transfer, cheque or UPI, and send the shop a receipt.

Dispatch and trip board

Pack, assign orders to vehicles and drivers, and track every trip from one board.

Driver and van-sale tools

Drivers see their own trips, deliver each stop and sell or take orders straight from the van.

Direct line to manufacturers

Receive manufacturer orders and brand deals, and keep your stock and catalogue up to date.

How It Works

From first call to first order.

STEP 1

Apply

Fill in the form with your business, products and the areas you serve.

STEP 2

Map your shops

Add the retailers you supply, or map shops already on RoadMate.

STEP 3

Set up your team

Add field executives, dispatchers and drivers with their own roles.

STEP 4

Run it digitally

Take orders, dispatch trips, collect dues and track stock in the app.
