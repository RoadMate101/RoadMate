For Manufacturers

Reach every shop that sells your brand.

Demand is moving through digital channels. RoadMate gives manufacturers direct visibility of their shops, the ability to book orders and run brand deals, and clear data on where their products sell.

Start Onboarding See the App
Benefits

See your whole market, down to the shelf.

RoadMate connects brands to retailers and distributors directly, with the insight and control to grow distribution.

Shelf-level coverage

See which shops stock your products and which have stopped, by product and by area.

Win back lost shops

Spot shops that dropped your products and plan visits to win them back first.

Book orders directly

Your field team books orders for shops and distributors in a few taps, with the shop owner confirming.

Brand deals and challenges

Launch brand deals and field-team challenges that reward new shops and first orders.

Choose your delivery route

Deliver through a shop’s usual distributor or ship direct, as suits the order.

Targets and insights

Track monthly targets, visits and coverage with data-driven distribution insight.
