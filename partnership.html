The Partnership Model

Own the territory. RoadMate runs the business.

RoadMate operates on a FOCO — Franchise-Owned, Company-Operated — model. Partners hold territorial rights and earn a share of revenue; RoadMate centrally manages technology, operations, marketing, staffing and training.
